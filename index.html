Corporate Identity

Culture is not enforced.
It permeates.

01

Connection (연결)

Understanding differs across environments. True connection requires time, patience, and the bridging of disparate thoughts.

02

Permeation (스며듬)

Our keyword. Culture should not be forced but should naturally diffuse into daily life through genuine appeal.

03

Continuity (이어짐)

K-Lifestyle becomes the standard, an object of admiration. We aim to make this way of life a global continuity.

The Standard

TOMIK Assessment Levels

Level 01

Basic Communication

Ability to communicate with Koreans using numbers and basic terminology. Essential for simple operational tasks.

Level 02

Academic Foundation

Understanding of most Korean mathematical terms. Capable of private tutoring for Korean elementary students.

Level 03

Advanced Proficiency

Full comprehension of middle & high school math problems. Capable of teaching and understanding corporate economic terminology.

Professional Certification

Business Math Instructor

Certified to teach Levels 1, 2, and 3. Qualification officially recognized by the Korea Math Ability Certification Association (K-MACA).
